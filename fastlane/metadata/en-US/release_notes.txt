New: Personalized learning levels

Professor Madrid now truly adapts to how much Spanish you know. Brand-new learners start mostly in their own language, with just a word or two of Spanish introduced at a time — no more being thrown into full Spanish sentences on day one. As you practice, the app gradually brings in more Spanish, at your pace.

New: Level settings

A new Level tab (Settings → Level) lets you see your current stage, fine-tune how much Spanish vs. your own language is used, turn on translation helpers, and get a friendly nudge on Home when you're ready to level up.

Sharper, more focused teaching moments in Speaking, Street View, and Roleplay conversations — each message now clearly teaches one word or phrase at a time for newer learners, instead of rushing past it.

Various polish and bug fixes.
